I'm a Master's in Design student at Indus University, Ahmedabad, building my path in UI/UX. I start by listening: surveys, interviews, empathy maps. Then I sketch, break things, and shape them into screens that feel obvious to use.

Right now I'm sharpening my research and service design skills. I'm looking for a first UI/UX internship where I can learn fast, ask good questions, and ship thoughtful work.

A few things about my design brain

  • People before pixels. Every project of mine opens with personas, empathy maps or journey maps.
  • I like everyday problems. Scalding tea cups, canteen queues, random toddler taps.
  • Fun and trust can share a screen. Blinkit for Kids is playful for kids and calm for parents.
  • I design things you can hold, too. Product and packaging sit happily next to apps.
